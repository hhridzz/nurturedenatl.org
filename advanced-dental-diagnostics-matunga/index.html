An accurate diagnosis is the foundation of considerate, minimally invasive dentistry. At Nurture Dental, Dr Ami Shah combines careful examination with appropriate digital imaging to understand what is happening beneath the surface, explain the findings and recommend treatment tailored to you.

Our Carestream CS 8200 3D

Our clinic uses the Carestream CS 8200 3D system for panoramic imaging (OPG) and three-dimensional cone beam CT (CBCT) when clinically indicated. Unlike flat radiographs, CBCT lets us review dental anatomy in multiple planes. It can be valuable for implant positioning, impacted wisdom teeth, complex root canals, selected bony lesions and surgical planning. An OPG gives a broad overview of the teeth and jaws, and segmented panoramic protocols can support specific dental examinations.

Focused endodontic imaging

A smaller scan volume, where available, can concentrate on a single tooth or a limited region. This can help investigate unusual canal anatomy, suspected missed canals, persistent periapical disease or a possible fracture. CBCT does not detect every crack, so image interpretation is always combined with clinical testing.

Choosing a field of view

The CS 8200 family offers several configurations. The scan area is chosen to answer the clinical question, always with radiation protection and the smallest suitable field of view in mind.

TMJ and jaw assessment

The machine supports panoramic TMJ projections, and selected CBCT studies can show the bony components of the jaw joints. Disc position and soft tissue structures may require MRI. We request imaging only when it will help clinical decisions.

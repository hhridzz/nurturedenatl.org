Prevention

Our preventive toolkit

Four layers of protection, tailored to age, exposure and individual needs.

Pit and fissure sealants

Deep grooves in back teeth can trap plaque and food. A protective sealant may be recommended, particularly for cavity-prone grooves in children and adolescents or other patients at risk. Sealants need periodic review and repair when necessary.

Fluoride applications

Professional fluoride varnishes or other appropriate applications can help strengthen enamel and reduce the risk of new cavities, especially in patients with higher caries risk. We tailor advice to age, exposure and individual needs.

Early enamel remineralisation

Some initial non-cavitated white-spot lesions can be managed by improving plaque control, reducing frequent sugar exposure and using remineralising products when appropriate. Professional remineralisation strips or other products may support the plan, but they cannot reliably repair a physically cavitated hole.

Your daily prevention plan

We discuss brushing with fluoride toothpaste, interdental cleaning, snacking patterns, dry mouth and regular reviews. Prevention is a shared, ongoing effort, not a single procedure.

When the whole smile needs a coordinated plan.

Extensive tooth wear, missing teeth, repeated breakages or difficulty chewing may involve more than one tooth. Full-mouth rehabilitation considers how teeth, muscles, joints and the bite function together, while taking appearance and long-term maintenance into account.

Planning before treating

We begin with a comprehensive examination, photographs, digital scans, relevant imaging and a discussion of your priorities. Selected patients may benefit from a Kois deprogrammer, which helps us assess jaw position and bite relationships by reducing habitual tooth-guided closure. We may record centric relation and assess whether a change in vertical dimension is appropriate.

Testing a new bite

Not everyone requires a raised bite. If an increase in vertical dimension is indicated, provisional restorations, mock-ups or other reversible stages may help evaluate comfort, function, speech and appearance before definitive work.

Your combination of treatments

A tailored sequence might include adhesive restorations, crowns, replacement of missing teeth with implants, gum care, orthodontics and preventive maintenance. We aim to preserve healthy tooth structure where possible and to stage complex care in manageable phases.

A partnership over time

Successful rehabilitation depends on diagnosis, planning, laboratory collaboration, regular reviews and home maintenance. A protective night guard may be advised in appropriate cases.

At a glance

Complex Care

The clinical journey

Six stages, from first assessment to long-term maintenance.

  1. 01

    Comprehensive diagnosis

    Dental assessment, intraoral scans, photographs and imaging as indicated.

  2. 02

    Bite and jaw assessment

    We evaluate centric relation, jaw function, wear and the existing bite.

  3. 03

    Kois deprogrammer and bite records

    When indicated, deprogramming and bite records help us plan a stable bite.

  4. 04

    Personalised treatment plan

    We assess whether changing vertical dimension is appropriate, and plan restorations and implants if needed.

  5. 05

    Provisional trial phase

    Comfort, speech, function and aesthetics are tested with provisional restorations where indicated.

  6. 06

    Definitive care and maintenance

    Planned restorations are delivered, with reviews, hygiene and protective appliances as needed.

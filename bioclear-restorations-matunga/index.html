Preserve more of your natural tooth.

Not every chipped, worn or heavily restored tooth needs a crown. When suitable, adhesive composite techniques can rebuild tooth form while preserving more natural structure than some full-coverage approaches.

What is the BioClear approach?

The BioClear method uses carefully adapted anatomical matrices and heated composite injection-moulding techniques in appropriate cases. Attention to isolation, bonding, contour and finishing helps create smooth transitions and well-shaped proximal contacts.

Closing gaps and rebuilding contours

Selected cases may involve black triangles, small gaps, worn edges or restorations that trap food because of poor contacts. We first assess why the problem developed, then decide whether composite repair is likely to give an acceptable functional and aesthetic result.

Strength without exaggerated promises

Bonded composite can be durable and conservative, but it is not always equivalent to a crown and cannot prevent root canal treatment when the pulp is already irreversibly damaged. Longevity depends on tooth structure, bite, habits, hygiene and maintenance.

Our philosophy: conserve sound enamel and dentine wherever feasible, use precision bonding and select the least invasive reliable option for each tooth.

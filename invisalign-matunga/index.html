A discreet way to move teeth.

Invisalign uses a planned series of removable clear aligners to guide tooth movement. It may be suitable for selected crowding, spacing and bite concerns. Every plan begins with an orthodontic assessment, never with a promise of a specific result.

Different needs at different ages

Children, teenagers and adults have different growth patterns, compliance needs and treatment goals. We discuss appropriate Invisalign options according to the stage of dental development and the complexity of the case, including whether braces would offer better control.

Life with aligners

Aligners are typically worn for most of the day and removed for meals and oral hygiene. Refinements, attachments, monitoring visits and retainers may be needed. Treatment duration varies with complexity and adherence.

Your consultation

We explain expected tooth movements, limitations, options and long-term retention, so you can make an informed decision about your smile.

The Invisalign journey

Four stages, from the first scan to a smile you keep.

  1. 01

    3D digital scan

    Our TRIOS 5 scanner captures digital impressions without conventional putty trays in suitable workflows. Clinical photos, radiographs when indicated and a bite evaluation allow us to plan and explain the proposed changes.

  2. 02

    Custom aligners

    A series of clear, removable aligners, designed for you.

  3. 03

    Wear and progress

    Gentle, planned tooth movement, with regular monitoring visits.

  4. 04

    Your new smile

    Straighter, healthier and more confident teeth, held in place with retainers.

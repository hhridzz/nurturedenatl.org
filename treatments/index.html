Our Treatments

Each page explains one treatment in plain language: what it is, who may need it, how it is performed, the alternatives, the risks, and what recovery looks like.

Guided Biofilm Therapy

A gentle, step-by-step professional cleaning that lifts plaque and stain.

Crowns & Bridges

Rebuild a damaged tooth or replace a missing one with a fixed restoration.

Dental Implants

Replace a missing tooth from the root up with a titanium post and a custom crown.

Veneers & Smile Makeovers

Reshape colour, size and symmetry with thin, bonded shells.

Laser & Gum Treatment

Precise soft-tissue care for gum problems and gum reshaping.

Scanning & Diagnostics

Digital impressions and 3D imaging to plan treatment precisely.

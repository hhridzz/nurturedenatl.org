From impressions to digital precision.

Traditional impressions can involve bulky trays and setting materials. Our 3Shape TRIOS 5 wireless intraoral scanner captures the surfaces of your teeth and bite digitally, with no trays to bite into. Digital impressions can support crowns, bridges, selected implant restorations, aligner workflows and monitoring.

A consultation you can see

Your appointment is never limited to a quick look in a hand mirror. We combine a structured 35-point oral health examination with relevant photographs, scans and imaging. On our large interactive screen we can magnify and annotate areas of interest, so you understand a worn edge, an open contact, a missing tooth or an area needing further examination.

Your treatment, explained

Our findings guide a personalised plan, with options, sequencing and costs discussed transparently. Not every patient needs every test: imaging is prescribed according to clinical need.

Why patients appreciate the difference

Digital records make communication with laboratories and specialists easier, and they let us compare changes over time. Most importantly, they invite you to take part in decisions about your own smile.

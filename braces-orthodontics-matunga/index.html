Orthodontics

Choose your appliance

Three families of braces, each with its own character.

Traditional metal braces

Metal brackets and wires are versatile and frequently used for children, teenagers and adults. They are durable and offer controlled tooth movement.

Ceramic braces

Tooth-coloured ceramic brackets are less conspicuous than conventional metal brackets, although they can be more fragile or require particular care.

Self-ligating and Damon systems

Self-ligating brackets use a clip or door mechanism to hold the wire instead of traditional elastic ties. Damon is a branded self-ligating system. These appliances come in different material styles, and treatment time and comfort depend on the case, not the bracket label alone.

A more thoughtful approach to cleaning.

Plaque is a sticky bacterial biofilm that can contribute to cavities, gum inflammation and periodontal disease. Guided Biofilm Therapy, or GBT, is a structured preventive approach that aims to make biofilm visible and remove it precisely, while minimising unnecessary instrumentation.

Designed around your mouth

GBT may be used as part of maintenance for natural teeth, implant restorations, braces and other dental work, with the technique adapted to the individual. Patients often appreciate the guided approach and the chance to see which areas need more attention at home.

An appointment with a comfort menu

The GBT experience is complemented by calming music and patient comforts, including eye patches and blankets when suitable. We want your preventive appointment to feel as reassuring as it is informative.

Is it right for me?

The interval between cleaning appointments depends on gum health, plaque control, smoking or other risk factors, and your medical and dental history. GBT supports prevention but does not replace treatment for advanced gum disease when that is required.

The eight steps of GBT

An internationally recognised framework, adapted to you.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Your dental professional evaluates the health of your gums.

  2. 02

    Disclose

    A disclosing agent shows exactly where biofilm collects.

  3. 03

    Motivate

    Personalised brushing and interdental-care advice, based on what has just been revealed.

  4. 04

    AIRFLOW

    Removes the disclosed deposits and stains.

  5. 05

    PERIOFLOW, when indicated

    Added where clinically appropriate for deeper pocket treatment.

  6. 06

    PIEZON instrumentation

    Calculus removal, added where clinically appropriate.

  7. 07

    Check

    A final review of the result and the condition of your gums.

  8. 08

    Recall

    A personalised return interval, set by your gum health, plaque control and risk factors.

Surgery guided by careful diagnosis.

An impacted wisdom tooth, a damaged tooth or an oral lesion may require surgical care. At Nurture Dental, we plan procedures with thorough history-taking, radiographic assessment when indicated and clear explanations of what recovery will involve.

Impacted and difficult wisdom teeth

When a wisdom tooth is partially or completely trapped in gum or bone, removal may involve raising a flap, carefully creating access, sectioning the tooth and placing sutures. CBCT may be considered if a conventional X-ray does not adequately clarify its proximity to important anatomical structures.

Other procedures

Depending on the clinical problem and team availability, surgical care may include selected tooth extractions, soft tissue management and coordinated specialist procedures. Our experienced clinicians and visiting specialists help guide case selection and referral when needed.

Comfort and aftercare

We discuss local anaesthesia, expected swelling and soreness, medication, dietary precautions and review visits. Recovery differs for every patient.

Contact the clinic promptly for worsening swelling, persistent bleeding, fever or other concerning symptoms.
